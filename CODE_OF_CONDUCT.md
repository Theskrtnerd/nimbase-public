# Community code of conduct

Nimbase is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community
for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, respect different
levels of experience, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Do
not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or persistently harmful. Report
conduct concerns privately to `nimbase.ai@gmail.com` with the subject “Conduct
report.” Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts
of interest avoided where possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
